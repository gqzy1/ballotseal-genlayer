# BallotSeal scope

BallotSeal focuses on the chronological relationship between proposal revisions and prior acknowledgements: monotonic consent epochs, immutable version and acknowledgement history, and independent five-dimensional material-change assessment. Generic voting, quorum and treasury execution are outside scope.

V0.3 includes immutable acknowledgement records, bounded explanations with grounded quotes, parent text digests and bounded storage. The repository makes no claim of worldwide novelty or uniqueness.
